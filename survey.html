The Idea

What if your sports audio followed the game?

LockIn TV would be a smart soundbar for live sports. It raises the volume for game action and lowers it for commercials, halftime, and downtime.

Buy the soundbar once, link your phone, pick your game, set your volume levels, and watch normally. You can multitask when needed and lock in when the important moments happen.
