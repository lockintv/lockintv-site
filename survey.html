The Idea

What if your sports audio followed the game?

LockIn TV would be a small smart speaker or soundbar for live sports. It would keep the volume up during game action, lower it during downtime, and let you set the levels from your phone or a simple remote. The goal is to keep the game on without constantly muting, unmuting, or missing big plays when you look away.
