The Idea

What if your sports audio followed the game?

LockIn TV would be a small smart speaker or soundbar for live sports. It would keep the volume up during game action and let you smoothly lower or mute commercials, halftime, and other downtime without constantly muting and unmuting your TV. You would control everything from your phone: set your preferred levels once, then multitask when needed and lock in for the important moments. The goal is simple: keep the game on without missing the moments that matter.
