FAQ

Questions about LockIn TV.

A quick overview of the product idea while we test demand, hardware options, and manufacturing paths.

What is LockIn TV?

LockIn TV is an early-stage product idea for live sports audio. The goal is to help your TV sound follow the rhythm of the game: louder for live action and important moments, lower for downtime and breaks, based on settings you control from your phone.

So it's a paid service?

The current direction is a one-time hardware purchase plus a monthly subscription. The hardware would likely be a smart soundbar or connected TV-audio device. The subscription would cover the live game-state service, app features, syncing, supported games, and ongoing improvements. Exact pricing is not final; that is one reason we are running the survey.

How does the phone app work?

The app would let you choose the sport or game you are watching, connect or control your LockIn TV device, and set your preferred volume levels for action, downtime, and breaks. Once set, the system would automatically adjust the TV audio during the game.

Can I use it with my streaming services?

The goal is yes: it should work with how you already watch live sports, including cable, antenna or local TV, and live streaming services or apps such as YouTube TV, Fubo, Hulu + Live TV, DirecTV Stream, Sling, ESPN, Peacock, Paramount+, Prime Video, Fox, CBS, NBC, and league packages where supported. The exact supported setup will depend on the final hardware design.

What if my stream is behind, or I pause the game?

Different TV sources can be ahead or behind each other. The planned app would include a sync step, similar in spirit to how Shazam recognizes audio. With permission, the app could briefly listen to the game audio, match where your TV feed is, and adjust timing so volume changes line up with what you are actually watching. If you pause or fall behind, you would be able to resync.
